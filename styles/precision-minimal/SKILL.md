# Precision Minimal

Use this style for factorial interactions, controlled comparisons, and compact empirical arguments.

## Visual language

- Reduce decoration and maximise alignment.
- Use repeated small multiples when conditions share a common structure.
- Map the first explanatory dimension to cobalt and the second to muted magenta.
- Make reversals visible through geometry and direct annotation, not colour alone.
- Keep axes, rules, and secondary marks quiet.

## Palette

| Role | Colour |
|---|---|
| primary dimension | #3157B7 |
| interacting dimension | #B44E87 |
| secondary cobalt | #99AEDC |
| text and structure | #343941 |
