# Causal Transfer Map

Use this style for causal stories that move from contrasting conditions through a mechanism to observable outputs.

## Visual language

- Make the causal direction unambiguous.
- Use crimson and teal for contrasting conditions.
- Use blue for the central effect and gold for a focal outcome class.
- Keep auxiliary measures or outputs in a quiet slate.
- Use a warm paper background and restrained diagram geometry.
