# Moe Scientific Figure Skills

Choose a style by the paper's argument and information structure, not by decorative preference.

## Route to a style

- State transitions, temporal stages, and before/after comparisons: read [Dynamic Interface](styles/dynamic-interface/SKILL.md).
- Distinct system layers, modules, or parallel mechanisms: read [Editorial Systems](styles/editorial-systems/SKILL.md).
- Controlled interactions, contrasts, crossovers, or reversals: read [Precision Minimal](styles/precision-minimal/SKILL.md).
- Alternative pathways, branching decisions, and categorical outcomes: read [Editorial Semantic](styles/editorial-semantic/SKILL.md).
- Conditions, causal transfer, mechanisms, and effect pathways: read [Causal Transfer Map](styles/causal-transfer-map/SKILL.md).
- Event chains, evidence strength, revisions, and outcome states: read [Evidence Map](styles/evidence-map/SKILL.md).

For rules shared by every style, read [scientific-figure-principles.md](references/scientific-figure-principles.md).

## Invariants

- Assign colour by scientific meaning and preserve that mapping across all figures.
- Never use one colour for unrelated constructs merely to balance the composition.
- Add shape, line style, labels, or position when colour alone would carry an essential distinction.
- Keep panel letters and figure numbers outside the artwork when the publication template supplies them.
- Inspect every figure at its final single- or double-column size.
- Prefer an explicit, small palette over decorative gradients and incidental colour.
